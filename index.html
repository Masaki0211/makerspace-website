What to include in your email

  • Your STL file(s) as attachments
  • Quantity of each file
  • Color for each file
  • Material for each file: ABS, PLA, TPU, etc.
  • Say that you are paying with a FOP
    Only if you plan to use a FOP. Otherwise, leave this out.
Failed prints are still charged

If a print fails, we will reprint it up to two more times. If every attempt fails, you take the failed print and still pay for it.

Please send a well-designed STL file so your print succeeds the first time.

How it works

  1. Send your files You

    Email your STL files to make@ttu.edu with the quantity, color, and material for each file. Mention FOP up front if you are using one.

  2. We prepare your quote Makerspace

    A student assistant slices your files to find the weight in grams, using KISSlicer for ABS and TPU or UltiMaker for PLA. The invoice adds material cost, a service fee for each printer run, and 8.25% sales tax.

  3. Approve or cancel by email You

    Review the invoice and reply to the same email thread. If you are paying with a FOP, attach it to your approval.

  4. Your job enters the queue Makerspace

    Once approved, your files are printed in order. When every part is done, you get a 3D print pickup email.

  5. Pay and pick up You

    Come to the Makerspace front desk in TTU Library Room 210, pay, and take your prints.

How to book

  1. Email the Makerspace You

    Send a request to make@ttu.edu saying you want to use the laser cutter or the Cricut machine.

  2. Your request goes to staff Makerspace

    A student assistant forwards your email to staff, and a staff member contacts you directly.

  3. Set a time You

    Agree on a reservation time with the staff member.

  4. Make it, then pay You

    Bring your materials and files to your session. You pay afterward at $2 per hour.
